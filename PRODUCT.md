# EliteStay Definition

**EliteStay is a digital living-accommodation platform that brings the entire accommodation journey into one place. It enables people to discover, evaluate, secure, and manage accommodation through a unified experience, while providing hosts with the tools to publish, manage, and operate their accommodation efficiently.**

Rather than focusing solely on short-term listings or long-term rentals, EliteStay optimizes for the accommodation lifecycle. It supports diverse accommodation use cases—whether someone is staying for a month, relocating for three months, studying for a semester, or residing for years.

## Product Vision

EliteStay aims to simplify and modernize the accommodation experience by connecting guests and hosts through a secure, intuitive, and scalable platform.

Its mission is to provide a unified system where people can:

- Discover suitable accommodation.
- Evaluate options with confidence.
- Request and secure accommodation.
- Manage their stay after moving in.
- Enable hosts to efficiently manage accommodation and residents.

## Core Product Domains

The platform is organized around six core domains:

- **Identity** — Authentication, profiles, guests, and hosts.
- **Inventory** — Accommodation, listings, media, amenities, flexible pricing, and availability.
- **Discovery** — Search, categories, recommendations, destinations, and saved listings.
- **Booking** — Booking requests, approvals, availability checks, and lifecycle.
- **Stay Management** — Active stays, occupancy, resident management, reviews, and related stay operations.
- **Hosting & Administration** — Listing management, dashboards, moderation, reporting, and platform configuration.

## Product Principle

Every feature in EliteStay should support at least one of the following goals:

- Help someone discover accommodation.
- Help someone evaluate accommodation.
- Help someone secure accommodation.
- Help someone manage accommodation.
- Help a host provide and manage accommodation.

If a feature does not meaningfully contribute to one of these goals, it should be carefully evaluated before becoming part of the platform.
